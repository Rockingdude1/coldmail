# Influencer Cold Mail Campaign - Skills & Rules

This is Category 1 of the 3-category outreach system. Category 2/3 (doctors, clinic and hybrid) live in `skills.md` / `doctors_sheet.csv`. Never cross-apply rules between the two campaigns, they have different product suites, word limits, and CTAs.

## Who This Applies To

Knowledge-giver influencers with high Instagram followings who regularly receive questions in their comments. NOT doctors (those follow skills.md). Target categories include:

- Finance (CAs, personal finance, investing, tax advisors)
- Legal (lawyers explaining rights, consumer law, property, divorce)
- Fitness coaches / nutritionists (non-doctor)
- Career / education counselors
- Relationship / mental wellness coaches
- Astrologers
- Tech reviewers
- Skincare / beauty educators (non-dermatologist)
- Parenting coaches (non-doctor)

**If influencer HAS a personal website:** 1 product
1. AI-Powered Instagram Automation

**If influencer has NO personal website:** 2 products
1. Your Own Website

## Product Descriptions

### AI-Powered Instagram Automation (Main Product)

Two things it does, always pitch both. The hate/abusive comment deletion pointer is permanently removed, do not use it anywhere, this product is now a 2-pointer pitch, not 3.

1. **Instant comment/DM replies** - When someone asks a question the influencer has already answered in a reel or post, they get the exact link automatically, before the influencer even sees the notification. No manual replies.

2. **Unanswered questions database** - Every question that comes in without an existing answer gets captured into a running database. The influencer can see what their audience genuinely wants to know next, and use it to decide what content to make.

### Your Own Website (Only if no personal website)

Angle: "You do not have a personal website right now. Anyone who Googles '[their name/handle]' lands on their Instagram profile or someone else's page, not a space you own and control. I can build you a clean, professional website that brings everything together: [list their specific things - content, courses, consulting, speaking, booking, chatbot]."

## Writing Rules

- NO em dashes anywhere, ever. Use commas, colons, or new sentences instead.
- Keep mails SHORT. Nobody reads long cold emails. Total mail: under 90 words for 1-product, under 120 for 2-product. Shorter is always better.
- The opening hook/pain-point line is ONE short sentence, not two. Cut it down to the sharpest possible version of their gap, no run-on sentences stacking multiple stats or clauses.
- Subject lines: MAX 7-8 words. Count every word including numbers.
- Tone: direct, confident, solution-oriented
- Open with the influencer's specific pain point (their numbers, their gap, their situation), one line only
- No filler phrases, no preamble, no "I hope this finds you well"
- Never repeat an influencer already in MEMORY.md, always check before researching
- Each product description must be hyper-personalized: reference their actual content, follower count, platform, post count, niche-specific question types, etc.
- The recurring comment question must be hyper-specific to their niche and content. No generic examples.
- No video demo link.

**For 1-product mails (AI-Powered Instagram Automation only):**
- No numbered headers. Use "So, what's the solution?" as a section break after the opening.
- After the break, write the 2 features (instant replies, unanswered questions database, no hate-comment pointer) as crisp single-line bullets (not paragraphs).
- No closer line ("Nobody in X is doing this"). End directly with the ReWired line + CTA.

**For 2-product mails (Website + AI-Powered Instagram Automation):**
- Use bold numbered headers with short personalized paragraphs, 1-2 lines each, not 2-3.
- No closer line. End directly with the Re-Wired line + CTA.

## AI-Powered Instagram Automation - How to Write This Section

Write two crisp single-line bullets. Each must be personalized. The hate/abusive comment deletion bullet is permanently cut, never include it.

**Bullet 1 - Instant replies:**
"Every Instagram comment asking a question you have already answered gets the exact reel link automatically."
- Always say "Instagram comment" specifically. Some creators are on YouTube, podcasts, Twitter — make clear this is Instagram automation.
- Personalize by referencing their content type (reel, post, video) if helpful.

**Bullet 2 - Unanswered questions database:**
"Every new question on Instagram gets logged into a database so you always know which topics your [X followers] need covered next."
- Personalize the follower count and audience descriptor (followers / investors / clients etc.).

## Research Checklist (for each influencer)

- [ ] Instagram handle + follower count
- [ ] YouTube channel + subscriber count (if present)
- [ ] Personal website (YES or NO, exact URL if yes)
- [ ] Public-facing email address
- [ ] Niche / content focus
- [ ] Most common recurring question type in their comments
- [ ] Notable content hooks (viral reels, book, course, podcast, consulting)
- [ ] Pain point angle: what specific gap exists between their content reach and comment management?
- [ ] Post count (to reference how much content is buried)

## Delivery

- If email found: create Gmail draft via Gmail MCP tool
- If no public email found: skip the influencer entirely. Do not create a local draft file, do not add them to the tracker. Only researched influencers with a usable email address get drafted.
- After creating draft: update MEMORY.md with influencer's full profile and draft status

## Subject Line Examples

Good formulas (same as doctor rules):
- [Big follower number]. [Contradiction or gap].
- [Their content stat]. [Problem in 3-4 words].
- [Niche hook]. [Gap in one phrase].

Examples:
- "890K followers. Comments still unanswered." (5 words)
- "500 tax reels. Questions still going nowhere." (7 words)
- "1.2M followers. Hate comments still manual." (6 words)
